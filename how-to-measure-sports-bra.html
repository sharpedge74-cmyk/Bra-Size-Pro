The Physics of Breast Motion During Exercise

Biomechanical research from the University of Portsmouth shows that unsupported breasts move in a complex figure-eight pattern during athletic exertion:

  • 50% of motion is vertical (up and down).
  • 25% of motion is lateral (side to side).
  • 25% of motion is anterior-posterior (forward and backward).

A standard compression crop top only addresses anterior movement. Encapsulation bras, which feature individual molded cups and cushioned underwires, control motion across all three planes of space.

Choosing Between Compression, Encapsulation, and Hybrid Bras

Style How It Works Best For Motion Reduction
Compression Presses breast tissue firmly against chest wall as a single mass. A-C cups; yoga, walking, cycling. Up to 50%
Encapsulation Encloses each breast separately in sculpted cups and individual wires. D+ cups; running, HIIT, horseback riding. Up to 83%
Hybrid Combines individual molded internal cups with an external compression overlay. All sizes; marathon running, CrossFit. Up to 85%
