Where Bra Support Truly Comes From

A properly engineered brassiere distributes weight through precise structural physics:

  • 85% to 90% of all support comes directly from the horizontal band anchored securely around the ribcage.
  • 10% to 15% of support comes from the shoulder straps (which primarily serve to shape the cup neckline).

When a band is too loose, the entire load transfers upward into the shoulder straps, which dig into trapezius muscles and cervical vertebrae, contributing to tension headaches, shoulder dents, and chronic upper back fatigue.

The 3-Point Underbust Measurement Method

  1. Loose Underbust: Tape is relaxed; skin is lightly touched.
  2. Snug Underbust: Moderate tension; your ideal band feeling.
  3. Tight Underbust: Exhale all air and pull the tape as tight as humanly possible without breaking it.

Compare snug and tight measurements: if the difference is 2.5 inches or more, your ribcage has soft tissue that compresses easily, meaning you will benefit from taking the smaller even band size for stability.
