Understanding fit

A Size Label Is Only Part of the Picture

A bra size combines a band number and a cup letter, but those labels do not describe every detail of a garment. Shape, fabric, stretch, and design can all affect how the same labeled size feels and fits.

What a label cannot tell you

  • How firm or stretchy a particular band feels.
  • Whether the cup shape matches the shape of the garment you are comparing.
  • How a brand's sizing differs from another brand's labels.

What these tools can explain

  • How measurements are turned into a starting size.
  • How cup letters relate to band sizes.
  • How common sizing systems and sister sizes compare.
