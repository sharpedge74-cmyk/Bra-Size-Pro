Fit Reality

Why Your Current Bra May Not Fit

Bra fit depends on the relationship between your ribcage, bust volume, cup depth, wire width, and band tension—not simply a letter and number.

× Common Retail Error

  • Band can be too loose and ride up the back.
  • Straps may compensate for an unsupported band.
  • Shallow or undersized cups can create spillage and pressure.

✓ IMRango Precision Method

  • Uses the snug underbust as the primary band anchor.
  • Checks bust-to-band difference for cup sizing.
  • Considers fit symptoms and international sizing systems.
