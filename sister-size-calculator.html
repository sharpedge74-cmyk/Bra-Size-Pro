How sister sizes are calculated

The matrix moves the band in 2-inch steps and moves the cup position in the opposite direction within the selected sizing-system sequence. This produces nearby label combinations commonly used as sister-size comparisons.

  1. Current size: the entered band and cup establish the reference position.
  2. One step: a 2-inch smaller band is paired with the next cup position; a 2-inch larger band is paired with the previous cup position.
  3. Two steps: the same relationship is extended to a 4-inch band change where the cup sequence allows it.

The matrix is a sizing-system relationship, not a promise that two garments will have identical physical cup dimensions. Wire geometry, cup shape, fabric, and construction can change the fit. Read the sister-sizing explanation.

How to use the matrix

  • Use sister sizes to compare adjacent labels when the band feels too loose or too firm and the cups otherwise seem close.
  • Recheck the whole garment. A different band can change how the cups sit and how the wires rest against the body.
  • Do not assume sister sizes have identical cup dimensions or feel identical across brands and styles.
  • The current selector supports the cup options displayed in the form. Some labels may not be available in every regional sequence; if an option cannot be mapped, the tool will ask you to choose a supported label.
