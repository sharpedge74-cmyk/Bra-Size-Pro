The Biggest Myth: Cup Size Has an Absolute Volume

Many people believe that an "A cup" is always small, a "C cup" is average, and a "D cup" is large. In truth, cup volume is completely relative to the band size.

Consider the volume of liquid that fills a bra cup:

  • 30D: Holds approximately 480 cc of tissue (Sister to 32C, 34B, 36A).
  • 34D: Holds approximately 710 cc of tissue (Sister to 36C, 38B, 40A).
  • 38D: Holds approximately 1000 cc of tissue.

A 38A cup actually holds more volume than a 30D cup!
