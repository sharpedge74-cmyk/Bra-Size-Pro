Direct answer

Use your current underbust and bust measurements to get a starting label, then compare it with the manufacturer's chart for the exact sports bra. Some sports bras use band-and-cup labels, while others use alpha sizes such as S, M, and L. The calculator's activity selector changes its general style guidance, not the size formula, and cannot measure the support performance of a garment.

Estimate a sports-bra starting point

Enter your underbust and full-bust measurements, then choose the activity you have in mind. The calculator estimates a bra-size label and offers general construction ideas to compare. Activity level does not change the calculated size, and the result cannot predict the performance of a specific sports bra.

How this sports-bra starting point is calculated

The size estimate uses the same measurement-based band and cup starting method as the main calculator: underbust determines the even-number band reference, while the bust-to-underbust difference determines the cup position.

  1. Band: the underbust is converted to the calculator's reference units and mapped to the nearest even band.
  2. Cup: bust minus underbust is rounded to a whole-inch cup index in the US sequence.
  3. Activity: your selected activity level changes the support-style guidance, not the calculated label.

Sports-bra research shows that breast movement and support needs vary with activity and individual circumstances, so the size label should be checked against the garment's own chart and fit. (Norris et al. 2020, Ergonomics; Exell et al. 2019, Sports Biomechanics)

Choose support by fit and activity, not label alone

  • Research testing 98 sports bras found differences in breast-movement reduction and identified garment characteristics associated with performance. The sample does not represent every sports bra. Read Norris et al. (2021).
  • The activity selector gives general guidance; it does not test a garment or measure breast movement.
  • Check the product's size chart, adjustability, comfort, and support while moving. Alpha sizes do not map to one universal band-and-cup size.
  • If exercise-related pain persists or you have a medical concern, seek advice from a qualified clinician.
