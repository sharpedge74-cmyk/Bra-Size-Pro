Direct answer

This calculator estimates a starting label for bra-sized bikini or tankini tops from underbust and bust measurements. It does not assign a universal one-piece swimsuit size. For one-pieces, compare your bust, waist, hip, and torso measurements with the manufacturer's current chart because a bra label alone does not describe the whole garment fit.

How the swimwear starting point is calculated

Bra-sized swimwear uses the same measurement-based band and cup starting relationship as the main calculator. One-piece guidance is handled separately because torso length and garment construction can make a simple bra-size mapping unreliable.

  1. Bra-sized piece: underbust establishes the band and bust minus underbust establishes the cup position.
  2. Torso: the shoulder-to-crotch loop is recorded as an additional fit reference for one-piece construction.
  3. One-piece result: the tool does not force a universal numeric size; it returns brand-dependent guidance where the available logic cannot support a reliable mapping.

Swimwear fabric, stretch, wet fit, torso proportions, and manufacturer sizing can affect the final choice. Check the current garment chart before purchase.

Worked example

Suppose your underbust is 34 inches and your bust is 38 inches. The difference is 38 − 34 = 4 inches. The calculator applies its band rule to the 34-inch underbust and maps the four-inch difference through its configured US cup sequence, producing a starting label. The one-piece result remains “Brand-dependent”; use your waist, hips, and torso loop with the exact product chart.
