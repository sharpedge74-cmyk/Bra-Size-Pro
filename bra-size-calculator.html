How this starting point is calculated

IMRango uses the snug underbust and standing bust measurements as the two inputs for the main calculation. Tight underbust and leaning bust are optional references and do not change the result.

  1. Band: snug underbust is converted to inches, rounded to the nearest whole inch, then moved to the next even number when needed.
  2. Cup index: standing bust minus snug underbust is rounded to the nearest whole inch.
  3. Label: the cup index is looked up in the selected US, UK, EU, or AU cup sequence.
  4. Conversion: common regional band labels come from the same canonical sizing data used by the calculator.

This is a reproducible sizing reference, not a universal fit guarantee. Manufacturer charts, garment construction, cup shape, fabric stretch, body shape, and fit preferences can change the size that works in a particular bra. See the full sizing methodology.
