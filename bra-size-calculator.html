How this starting point is calculated

Bra Size uses the snug underbust and standing bust measurements as the two inputs for the main calculation. Tight underbust and leaning bust are optional references and do not change the result.

  1. Band: snug underbust is converted to inches, rounded to the nearest whole inch, then moved to the next even number when needed.
  2. Cup index: standing bust minus snug underbust is rounded to the nearest whole inch.
  3. Label: the cup index is looked up in the selected US, UK, EU, or AU cup sequence.
  4. Conversion: common regional band labels come from the same canonical sizing data used by the calculator.

This is a reproducible sizing reference, not a universal fit guarantee. Manufacturer charts, garment construction, cup shape, fabric stretch, body shape, and fit preferences can change the size that works in a particular bra. See the full sizing methodology.

What this calculator can and cannot tell you

  • It can produce a repeatable label from the two required measurements and the selected cup sequence.
  • It does not model a particular bra's cup shape, wire width, stretch, cut, or construction.
  • Tight underbust and leaning bust are optional and do not change the current result.
  • If the measurements are outside the supported range, or bust is not greater than underbust, the tool asks you to check the inputs.
  • If you have persistent pain, a new lump, or questions related to pregnancy or surgery, consult a qualified clinician. This calculator is not a medical tool.

Some sizing methods add inches to underbust measurements; others use different rounding rules. BraSizePRO uses the formula documented here and on the sizing methodology page. We do not label every alternative method outdated without a source that supports that exact claim.
