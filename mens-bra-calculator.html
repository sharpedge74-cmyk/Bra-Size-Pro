Direct answer

This calculator uses underbust and full chest measurements to estimate a band-and-cup starting label. It does not apply a gender-specific correction. The broad or slender torso option changes the style guidance only. Treat the result as a reference, then check the exact garment's size chart, construction, and comfort.

How this chest-fit starting point is calculated

The calculator uses the same measurement-based band and cup relationship as the general bra calculator. It does not apply a sex-specific correction to the measurements.

  1. Band: underbust is mapped to the nearest even band in the calculator's reference range.
  2. Cup: full chest measurement minus underbust determines the US cup index.
  3. Torso frame: the broad/slender selection affects the style guidance only; it does not change the calculated label.

Chest and breast shape vary substantially between people. Use the result as a starting reference and compare it with the product's sizing chart, construction, and fit.

Worked example

Suppose the underbust measurement is 38 inches and the full chest measurement is 40 inches. The difference is 40 − 38 = 2 inches. The calculator maps the 38-inch underbust to its band starting label and the 2-inch difference to the configured US cup sequence. This example follows the documented formula; the result is not a guarantee that every garment with that label will fit.
