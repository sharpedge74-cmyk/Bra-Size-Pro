What You'll Find Here

Bra Size brings bra-size calculators, measurement charts, and plain-language guides together in one place. The site explains how band numbers and cup letters work, how common sizing systems differ, and how measurements are used to produce a size reference.

The aim is to make a confusing set of labels easier to understand. Each tool focuses on a specific task, from calculating a starting size to comparing related sizes or reading a brand's size labels.

How the Tools Work

Calculators use the measurements and sizing rules described on their pages. Where a result depends on a particular cup sequence or band conversion, the relevant guide explains that approach. The Bra Sizing Methodology documents the main calculator's inputs and calculation steps.

A calculated result is a reference, not a promise that every garment carrying that label will fit in the same way. Brands can use different size charts, and garment shape, fabric, stretch, and construction can change how a labeled size feels.

Independent Sizing Information

Bra Size is an independent sizing-information website, not a bra manufacturer or retailer. Brand names and sizing details are included to help readers compare labels. They are not endorsements, and brand-specific information may change as product ranges and charts are updated.

Clear Scope and Limitations

Measurements are useful, but they cannot describe every detail of a garment or how it will fit. Different measurement methods and sizing conventions can produce different results. For that reason, the site distinguishes between the arithmetic a calculator performs, the labels used by sizing systems, and the fit of a particular product.

Content is written to explain clothing sizes in everyday language. The site does not present a calculated label as a medical finding or claim that one formula applies to every brand.

Keeping Information Useful

Pages are organized around specific sizing questions and link to related calculators, charts, and explanations. When a method or reference is important to understanding a result, the related page describes it so readers can follow the calculation rather than rely on an unexplained answer.
