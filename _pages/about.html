Our Story & Philosophy

Over 80% of individuals who wear bras are wearing an incorrect size. The vast majority are trapped in a band that is one to three sizes too loose and cups that are two to four sizes too small. The consequences aren't merely cosmetic: ill-fitting bras cause chronic shoulder indentations, trapezius headaches, pinched nerve pathways, and premature skin breakdown.

IMRango (iamrango.com) was founded with a singular purpose: to replace commercial sales gimmicks (like the archaic +4 inches method) with anatomical truth, physics-based load distribution, and open sizing education.

Independent & Unbiased Fit Intelligence

Unlike retail bra stores that manipulate sizing recommendations to fit customer bodies into whatever inventory happens to be on their shelves, IMRango is completely independent. Our sizing engines evaluate measurements against true international engineering standards (US, UK, EU, AU, FR, IT, and Japanese criteria).

Our Team

Our fit methodology is continuously reviewed by certified master bra fitters, apparel pattern engineers, and ergonomics researchers to ensure our recommendations account for breast projection, tissue density, root width, and asymmetric fullness.
