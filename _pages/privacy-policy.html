Your Privacy & Body Measurements

At IMRango (iamrango.com), your privacy is paramount. We believe personal body measurements are sensitive, intimate data.

100% Client-Side Calculations

All measurements entered into our calculators (underbust, standing bust, leaning bust, waist, hips) are processed entirely within your web browser using client-side JavaScript. Your measurements are never transmitted to, collected by, or stored on our web servers.

Local Storage & "Clear My Data" Control

If you choose to retain your calculations between page visits, data is stored solely in your personal browser's localStorage. You can wipe all stored data at any time by clicking the prominent "Clear my data" button in our navigation header or footer.

Cookies, Analytics & Advertising

The current site code does not include a first-party analytics tracker. Advertising may be displayed through the site's ad placements when a live advertising provider is enabled; that provider may use its own cookies or similar technologies subject to its own policies.
