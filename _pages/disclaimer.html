Educational & Informational Purpose Only

All content, calculators, sizing converters, diagnostic guides, and tools provided on IMRango (iamrango.com) are designed strictly for educational, informational, and general consumer fitting purposes.

Not Medical Advice

The information provided on IMRango does not constitute medical, orthopedic, or physical therapy advice and should not replace consultation with a qualified medical professional. If you experience severe thoracic pain, nerve tingling in your arms, rib fractures, mastitis, or breast lumps, consult a licensed healthcare provider immediately.

Commercial Fit Discrepancies

While our mathematical algorithms adhere to international sizing standards, individual garment manufacturers use proprietary fit models, differing wire tempers, and varying fabric elasticities. As such, calculated sizes serve as an optimal starting baseline.
