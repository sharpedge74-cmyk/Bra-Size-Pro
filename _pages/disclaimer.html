Educational & Informational Purpose Only

All content, calculators, sizing converters, fit guides, and tools provided by Bra Size are intended for general educational and consumer sizing purposes.

Not Medical Advice

Bra Size does not provide medical, orthopedic, physical-therapy, or diagnostic advice. A calculator result should not be used to diagnose or treat pain, injury, breast conditions, or another health problem.

If you have persistent or significant pain, numbness, skin injury, a new breast lump, signs of infection, or another concerning symptom, seek advice from an appropriately qualified healthcare professional.

Sizing Limitations

Our calculations are measurement-based references rather than a universal formula for every bra. Anthropometric standards define measurement terminology and methods, while commercial bra sizing and garment fit can vary between systems, manufacturers, and styles.

Use a calculated result as a starting point and compare it with the manufacturer's current size chart and the fit of the specific garment.
