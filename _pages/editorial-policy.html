Our Editorial Aim

Bra Size publishes practical explanations of bra sizing, measurement-based calculators, size charts, and comparisons between common sizing systems. Content is written in clear, everyday language and separates what a calculation does from what a size label means for a specific garment.

How We Explain Sizing

  1. Show the method: Where a calculator follows a defined formula or label sequence, the relevant page explains the inputs and main steps.
  2. Describe results accurately: Calculated sizes are presented as starting references, not as guaranteed matches for every bra or brand.
  3. Respect system differences: US, UK, EU, Australian and other sizing labels are not assumed to be interchangeable in every range. Conversions are described as references when systems differ.
  4. Keep brand details in context: Brand size charts and garment designs can change. Brand-specific information is comparative, not an endorsement or a guarantee of fit.
  5. Use sources carefully: External standards and published material are included only where they help explain a sizing convention, measurement term, or stated limitation.
  6. Write for people: Pages are intended to answer a distinct sizing question, avoid repetitive filler, and connect readers with relevant tools and related explanations.

What Our Tools Do Not Claim

A measurement-based result cannot account for every feature of a finished garment. Bra Size does not claim that one formula works for all manufacturers, that a size label is universal, or that a conversion guarantees an identical fit.

Corrections and Updates

When a calculation, size mapping, brand reference, or explanation changes, the relevant page can be updated to reflect the current information. Because brand ranges and sizing charts may change, the date shown on a page is a content-review marker rather than a guarantee that every external chart remains unchanged.

What We Avoid

Bra Size does not publish invented credentials, fabricated testimonials, fake ratings, or unsupported claims about brands. We avoid presenting a clothing-size calculation as a medical assessment and aim to explain uncertainty plainly when sizing conventions differ.
