How this strapless bra starting point is calculated

The calculator uses the same core measurement method as the universal bra calculator. It does not claim that every strapless bra needs a smaller size; instead, it gives you a consistent size starting point and then highlights the fit factors that matter most when straps are removed.

  1. Band: the snug underbust is converted to inches, rounded to the nearest whole inch, then moved to the next even number when needed.
  2. Cup index: standing bust minus snug underbust is rounded to the nearest whole inch.
  3. Label: the cup index is mapped to the selected US, UK, EU, or AU cup sequence.
  4. Strapless fit: the result is checked using band and sister-size guidance rather than applying an arbitrary size reduction.

A strapless bra can fit differently from a regular bra because support comes from the band, cups, wings, grip materials, and garment construction. Use the result as a starting point and confirm the manufacturer's own chart before buying. See the full sizing methodology.

Strapless fit checklist

  • Check that the band feels secure and stays reasonably level without painful pressure.
  • Check cup coverage and whether the top edge cuts in or leaves unwanted space.
  • Move your arms and sit naturally to see whether the garment shifts.
  • Check the product's instructions for grip strips, boning, and care.
  • Do not assume sizing down is required; compare fit and the manufacturer's chart.

Measurement-based methods can give different recommendations from professional fit criteria in some samples. Use the calculator as a starting point, then assess the actual garment. Read White & Scurr (2012). For the core formula, see Bra Sizing Methodology.
