Compare a size label before shopping

This converter maps a baseline US cup label to the general US or UK sizing sequence assigned to the selected brand. It does not read every product's chart or predict style-specific fit. Use the result as a comparison, then check the brand's current chart for the exact product and market.

How the brand comparison works

The converter treats your entered size as a baseline US label and maps its cup position into the selected brand's stated sizing system. The band number is retained as the baseline comparison.

  1. Baseline: the selected US cup is located in Bra Size's canonical cup sequence.
  2. System mapping: that cup position is read against the selected brand's declared US or UK system.
  3. Brand note: the result does not model every style. Use the brand's current product chart for the specific garment before ordering.

Manufacturer sizing can vary even when labels match; research has documented measurable differences in cup volume between manufacturers. (Moscarelli et al.)

How to verify a brand result

  1. Open the brand's official chart for the market where you plan to shop.
  2. Check whether the chart is for the same garment category. Bra, wireless bra, bralette, sports bra, swimwear, and shapewear charts may differ.
  3. Compare the result with the chart for the exact style where one is provided.
  4. Use the brand's own guidance if its chart does not support your size or the converter's label.

Limits of this converter

  • It maps a cup position between the site's general US and UK sequences and retains the entered band number.
  • It does not read live product inventory, select a bra style, or model fit for a specific garment.
  • It does not use every brand's product chart as an individual conversion table.
  • Manufacturer sizing and garment construction can differ. A peer-reviewed 2025 study measured cup-volume variation among bras from seven manufacturers in the sizes tested; the findings do not cover every brand, size, or style. Read the study abstract.
