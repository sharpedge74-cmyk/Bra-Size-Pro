How the brand comparison works

The converter treats your entered size as a baseline US label and maps its cup position into the selected brand's stated sizing system. The band number is retained as the baseline comparison.

  1. Baseline: the selected US cup is located in BraSizePRO's canonical cup sequence.
  2. System mapping: that cup position is read against the selected brand's declared US or UK system.
  3. Brand note: the result does not model every style. Use the brand's current product chart for the specific garment before ordering.
