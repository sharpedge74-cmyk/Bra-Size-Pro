Measurement-Based Sizing

A bra size combines a band label with a cup label. A measurement calculator can provide a useful starting point, but it cannot determine garment fit by numbers alone. ISO 8559-1:2017 standardizes definitions for clothing anthropometric measurements; it does not establish one universal bra-size formula.

Band Starting Point

Measure the underbust according to the instructions for the sizing system being used, then apply that system's band convention. Avoid presenting a single rounding rule as universal because commercial size charts differ.

Cup Starting Point

A simple method is to compare the full-bust and underbust measurements. This site uses approximately one cup step per inch of difference for its basic calculator. That is a starting convention, not a universal industry standard.
