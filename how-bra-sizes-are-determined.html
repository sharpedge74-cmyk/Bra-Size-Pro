The Modern Bra Sizing Formula

A bra size consists of two distinct components: the Band Size (the foundation that anchors the bra around your torso) and the Cup Size (the volume needed to enclose each breast comfortably).

Band Formula

Measure your snug ribcage underbust in inches.

  • If the measurement is an even number (e.g. 32.0"), your band size is 32.
  • If the measurement is an odd number (e.g. 33.0"), check your tight underbust. If you have soft tissue or prefer firm support, round down to 32. If you have a bony, muscular ribcage with little compression, round up to 34.
