Direct answer

This tool estimates a bra starting label from underbust and bust measurements, then selects a separate panty reference size from your hip measurement. It does not assume that a bra size predicts a bottom size. Treat both results as starting points and compare them with the current chart for the exact set or separates you plan to buy.

How the matching-set sizes are calculated

The tool calculates the bra top and bottom independently because coordinated lingerie sets do not necessarily use the same size scale for both pieces.

  1. Bra: underbust establishes the band and the bust-to-underbust difference establishes the cup position.
  2. Bottom: hip measurement is matched against the canonical panty sizing data.
  3. Coordination: the two results are displayed together so you can identify when a fixed S/M/L set may not match both measurements.

If a brand offers separates, the independently selected sizes may provide a closer starting point. Always compare both measurements with the brand's current chart.

Worked example

Suppose your underbust is 32 inches, full bust is 36 inches, and hips are 39 inches. The bust difference is 36 − 32 = 4 inches. The tool applies its band rule to 32 inches and maps the four-inch difference to its configured US cup sequence. A 39-inch hip measurement falls in the reference table's M range of 38–39 inches. These are separate starting references, not a guarantee that every matching set will fit.
