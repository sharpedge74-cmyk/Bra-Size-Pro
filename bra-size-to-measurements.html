Estimate reference measurements from a size label

Choose a US band and cup label to see the ranges produced by BraSizePRO's reverse-lookup formula. These are calculated references, not measured body dimensions, official standard ranges, or promises about fit. Use them to understand the site's formula, then check the chart for the exact bra you are considering.

How the reference measurements are estimated

This tool reverses Bra Size's simplified cup-index relationship to produce a measurement range associated with a US size label. It is a reference lookup, not a claim that everyone wearing the same label has the same body dimensions.

  1. Underbust: the entered band is represented as a range of one inch below to one inch above the band number.
  2. Bust: the cup index is added to the band and a half-inch margin is applied around that reference.
  3. Units: the resulting reference values can be displayed in inches or converted to centimeters.

Body proportions, measurement technique, garment construction, and manufacturer sizing can all produce different real-world fits. See the full methodology.

How to read these ranges

  • This is a BraSizePRO reference calculation, not a published body-measurement standard.
  • The tool accepts even band numbers from 28 through 50 and the cup labels in its selector.
  • It uses the cup index from the stored US sequence; other brands and systems may use different sequences.
  • Centimeter output rounds each endpoint to a whole centimeter, so converting back to inches may not reproduce the original value exactly.
